## Locked design decisions (Slice 2, Fable-planned, Olly-approved)
- **Consent + agreed rate ⇒ CONTRACTED** (not FIRM). Finance already prices CONTRACTED into the floor with `p90_revenue_haircut_contracted_pct` + contracted-basis DSCR — lender-coherent, honours the prompt's "flows to pricing + proposal" without overstating a merchant stream as risk-free. **Never-FIRM (all 4 layers) stays intact**; only `GatedUpsideStream`'s never-OPEN rule is relaxed, and ONLY when an explicit consent basis is supplied. (If literal FIRM is later insisted: one-line `open_firmness` swap.)
- **Consent reaches gates by reading `site.stream_consents` directly in `gates.py`** (same pattern as `has_export_consent`), NOT via GateContext (which is reserved for engine/time/fleet facts and drops on the unsolved path).
- **Consent is authorisation, not value:** consent without the needed rate/volume never creates £ or elevates firmness; the "consent given" label is orthogonal (shows regardless of whether the stream priced).
- **Estimation ladder:** real contracted > agreed rate (`agreed_rate_gbp` / ExportArrangement rate) > sourced/assumption > £0. Volumes always engine-measured. No invented numbers.
- **Placement:** a consented+priced (CONTRACTED) gated stream moves from `gated_upside` into `value_streams` (finance sums value_streams). Unconsented gated streams stay in `gated_upside`.
- **CM keeps deliverability physics** (consent can't bypass `deliverability_confirmed`). WHOLESALE: contracted £ = agreed export tariff × overlay export MWh; the MID perfect-foresight delta stays indicative/CONDITIONAL.
