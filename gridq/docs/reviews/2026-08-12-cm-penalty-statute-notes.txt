CM PENALTY RATE VERIFICATION — primary-source evidence (read-only investigation, 2026-08-13)
Source of truth: legislation.gov.uk revised (consolidated) text of SI 2014/2043.
NOTE: all formulae on legislation.gov.uk are GIF images, not text. I downloaded the GIFs,
upscaled them, and read them directly. Image files kept under ./leg/img/.

--- 1. THE PENALTY RATE (verbatim) ---

Sch 1 para 5(2A) [inserted 14.7.2016 by SI 2016/742 reg 1(2) Sch 1 para 33(2)]:
  "For the purpose of sub-paragraph (2), 'PRij' is the penalty rate in pounds per MWh applying
   to CMU i in respect of settlement period j, and is to be calculated in accordance with the
   formula—"   [img epp_017]  PR_ij = Σ_N (PR_N × ICO_ijN) / Σ_N ICO_ijN
  "where— 'ICOijN' is the whole or a part of any capacity obligation COzx applying to CMU i in
   settlement period j, and 'PRN' is the penalty rate applying to that capacity obligation."

Sch 1 para 5(3) [substituted 14.7.2016 by SI 2016/742 reg 1(2) Sch 1 para 33(3)]:
  "For the purpose of sub-paragraph (2A), PRN is to be calculated in accordance with the formula—"
  [img epp_018, read directly]  PR_N = PE_zx × (1/24)

Sch 1 para 5(4) [words substituted 14.7.2016 SI 2016/742 para 33(4); further 23.7.2025 SI 2025/917 Sch para 18(b)]:
  "'PEzx' means the price in pounds per MW determined for COzx in accordance with paragraph 3(4) to (5)"

Sch 1 para 5(2) [formula amended 14.7.2016]:  [img 052]  SPP_ij = PR_ij × (ALFCO_ij − AE_ij)

AS-MADE 2014 version, para 5(3), image 054, read directly:  PR_ix = PE_ix × 1/24.
=> THE DIVISOR HAS ALWAYS BEEN 24. It was never 4 in any version of this provision.

Changes-to-legislation check: the ONLY recorded change to Sch 1 para 5(3) is the SI 2016/742
substitution (applied, "Yes"). No prospective/unapplied amendment.

--- 2. WHAT PE IS (units chain) ---
Sch 1 para 3(2): [img epp_026]  ACP_yx = CO_yx × PE_yx   (ACP = ANNUAL capacity payment, £)
Sch 1 para 3(4): "'PEyx' means the price in pounds per MW at which capacity payments are payable
  in respect of COyx, which is to be determined in accordance with sub-paragraph (5)."
Sch 1 para 3(5): "If the relevant capacity auction was a T-4 auction, PE is to be calculated in
  accordance with the formula—" [img 038]  PE_ix = CCP_i × (CPI_x / CPI_base)
  ("CCPy" = "the capacity cleared price for COyx, as recorded on the capacity market register")
=> PE is £/MW PER DELIVERY YEAR, CPI-indexed from the auction base period to the delivery year.
=> PR = PE/24 in £/MWh. The 24 is a bare policy divisor, not a units conversion.

AS-MADE para 3(6): "If the relevant capacity auction was a T-1 auction or a DSR transitional
auction, PE_ix is equal to CCP_i."  OMITTED 23.7.2025 by SI 2025/917 Sch para 18(a)(ii); para
3(4) and 5(4) cross-refs narrowed to "(5)". Current Sch 1 para 3 mentions T-1 nowhere.
(Para 6A(3) still cross-refers to "paragraph 3(4) to (6)" — stale internal reference.)

--- 3. THE CAPS (verbatim) ---
Sch 1 does NOT hard-code the percentages. Para 6(6):
  "'Fy' (for any value y) means the percentage stated on the capacity market register under
   regulation 31(2)(f), at the date of issue of the capacity agreement, as the monthly penalty
   cap percentage for the capacity obligation COyx awarded in respect of CMU y for year X;
   'Gy' ... as the annual penalty cap percentage ..."
Sch 1 para 6(4) [substituted 14.7.2016]: [img epp_020] MPC_ij = RMCP_ij + Σ ASPPA′
  (a) [img epp_021] RMCP_ij = (ACP_ix × WF_mx × F_i) + Σ_N (tACP_N × WF_mx × F_z)
Sch 1 para 6(5A): [img epp_024] APC_ij = ACP_ix × G_i + Σ_N (tACP_N × G_z × WF_mx × DT_mN/D_m)
Sch 1 para 6(3): [img epp_019] P_ij = (SP_i / MaxSP_i) × min(MaxSP_i, MPC_ij)
Sch 1 para 6(5): [img epp_023] Q_ij = APC_ij − Σ_{k=1}^{m−1} MPSA_ik

reg 31(2)(f): "the annual penalty cap and monthly penalty cap applicable in accordance with the
  electricity capacity regulations in force at the date of issue of the capacity agreement,
  expressed respectively as percentages of the annual capacity payment and the monthly capacity
  payments payable under the capacity agreement"

reg 41(3A) [inserted 18.12.2014 by SI 2014/3354 reg 1(2) Sch 2 para 5]:
  "(3A) The percentages to be included in the capacity market register under regulation 31(2)(f)
   are— (a) for the annual penalty cap, 100%; and (b) for the monthly penalty cap, 200%."
=> BOTH CAPS CONFIRMED IN PRIMARY LEGISLATION. Carried by reg 41(3A), NOT by Sch 1 para 6(4).
No prospective/unapplied amendment to reg 41(3A).

CONDITIONALITY OF THE ANNUAL CAP — Sch 1 para 6(2)/(2A) [substituted 14.7.2016]:
  "(2) ... (a) SPPSAij is— (i) equal to the amount Pij ... unless paragraph (2A) applies; and
   (ii) equal to the lesser of the amount Pij and the amount Qij ... if paragraph (2A) applies"
  "(2A) This paragraph applies where— (a) a capacity provider penalty charge has been incurred in
   respect of CMU i in at least 48 relevant settlement periods, and (b) those periods together
   comprise at least 8 relevant settlement periods in each of at least 6 months in delivery year X."
=> The 100% ANNUAL cap only binds once that 48-period / 8-per-month / 6-month threshold is met.
   Below it only the 200% monthly cap applies.

--- 4. WHERE 0.25 COMES FROM ---
CMS legal update "The Capacity Market: What next?", 16 January 2023, describing the BEIS
consultation on CM reforms (closed 3 March 2023):
  current: "Penalty rate (expressed in £/MWh) = clearing price (£/MW) x 1/24"
  proposed: "clearing price (£/MW) x 1/4 instead of 1/24" — "BEIS proposes to significantly
  increase the penalty sixfold"; "there are no plans to change the penalty caps at present";
  earliest implementation 1 Oct 2024 (T-1) / 1 Oct 2027 (T-4).
=> 0.25 IS A REAL NUMBER: the 2023 PROPOSED rate. NEVER ENACTED. gb_v1_7's own source line for
   0.25 cites "CMS ... summaries" — same article. So the key is a proposal mislabelled as law.

NOT REVIVED / NOT PENDING:
- SI 2026/850 (The Electricity Capacity (Amendment and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2026),
  the only outstanding-not-yet-applied change to SI 2014/2043: amends regs 2, 16, 21, 32, 34, 43,
  51, 52, 53, 59, 60, inserts 59A. Explanatory Note: prequalification, credit cover, TERMINATION
  FEES ("higher termination fee rates"), CFD interaction, auction guidelines, reconciliation runs.
  It does NOT touch Sch 1 para 5 or 6, and does NOT touch reg 41.
- CMS, "Capacity market consultation - The 2026 shake-up", 12 Dec 2025: delivery-assurance
  tightening = termination fees (+~30% for inflation), credit cover, director attestations.
  No change to the penalty rate or the caps.
