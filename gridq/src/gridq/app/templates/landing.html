The opportunity

Behind every commercial site is a battery business case hiding in its half-hourly data.

The hard part was never the battery - it was sizing it and underwriting it credibly enough to finance. {# ========== THE PIPELINE, IN THE OPEN (7-stage pinned scroll walkthrough) ========== Each stage shows IN (what goes in), the process (what happens), and OUT (what comes out). No black box: this is the literal module sequence the engine runs, in order. All copy is qualitative or record-backed; the interface never invents a figure. #}
The pipeline, in the open
IntakeSite modelValue stackDispatchSizingFinanceQuote
01

Intake: your site, as evidence

InA half-hourly meter file if you have one; if not, the facts off your bills: annual usage, day and night volumes, observed peak, unit rates, levy lines, capacity charge, connection limits, consents.
HappensEverything is transcribed into one validated site record. Nothing is invented: with no meter file, a load profile is modelled from your stated facts and labelled modelled, never passed off as metered.
OutA graded site record (T0 to T2) plus a ranked list of exactly what is missing and what each gap blocks.
02

Data grading: how much can be trusted

InThe site record and its provenance: metered or modelled, full year or partial, confirmed or stated.
HappensEach input is graded and every downstream figure inherits the grade. A modelled profile can produce an estimate; only validated metered data can ever support a bankable quote.
OutThe data tier stamped on the run, and the honesty labels every later page carries.
03

Value stack: which revenues are real here

InYour tariff structure, DUoS band exposure, export consents, route-to-market status.
HappensEvery revenue stream is gated for THIS site: the firm floor (bill savings, DUoS red-band avoidance) is separated from conditional streams (Capacity Market, local flexibility) and from gated upside (wholesale, balancing) which never enters the priced floor.
OutThe open and gated stream map, with each gate's unlock condition stated.
04

Dispatch: a year, half hour by half hour

InThe load profile and your own prices placed in time: unit rates, DUoS bands, levy lines.
HappensAn optimiser schedules the battery across every half hour of a full year: charge cheap, discharge dear, never exceed the connection, respect warranty cycle limits, and keep the residual peak down. Deterministic: same inputs, same schedule, every time.
OutThe dispatch schedule, per-stream value, and your bill before and after.
05

Sizing: every size fights for the site

InA search space of power and duration scaled to your site's peak and connection.
HappensA staged continuous search runs the same dispatch at over a hundred candidate sizes and underwrites each with the same engine that underwrites the winner, so the search can never pick a system its own underwriting would reject.
OutThe full sizing surface as evidence, and the selected system. When nothing stacks up, the verdict says so plainly: no viable configuration, do not proceed. That honesty is the product.
06

Finance: lender arithmetic, in the open

InThe chosen system's revenue, and a versioned assumption release: capex, opex, degradation, haircuts. Every value carries its status; unconfirmed values are labelled.
HappensP50 and P90 cashflows are built, debt is sculpted, DSCR is measured against the covenant, and the price needed to hit the target return is checked against what the site already pays.
OutThe financial case: returns, payback, covenant verdict, and a price grounded in your real alternative.
